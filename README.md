### DevOps & Automation · Infrastructure · Software Development

## 💡 Engineering Mindset

> **Secure by design. Reliable by default. Automated wherever possible. Built to scale.**

I believe in engineering systems that are secure, reliable, and built to last.

I care about:

* 🔐 **Security by design** — reducing risks and building secure systems from the ground up
* 🛡️ **Reliability & Resilience** — designing infrastructure that remains stable and recoverable
* 📈 **Scalability** — building systems that can grow without becoming fragile
* 🤖 **Automation First** — eliminating repetitive tasks and making operations reproducible
* ⚙️ **Infrastructure as Code** — managing infrastructure through version-controlled, maintainable configurations
* 🧩 **Simplicity & Maintainability** — favoring clear, practical, and sustainable solutions
* 🔄 **Continuous Improvement** — refining systems, processes, and developer experience over time
